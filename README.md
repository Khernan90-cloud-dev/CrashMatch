## Overview
8
 
9
CrashMatch is a comparison platform inspired by Trivago that helps drivers find and compare accident-related services after a vehicle collision.
10
 
11
Instead of spending hours researching providers, users can compare trusted professionals in one place, including attorneys, insurance claim assistance providers, and collision repair shops. CrashMatch simplifies the recovery process by providing transparent information, ratings, pricing estimates, and service comparisons to help users make informed decisions.
