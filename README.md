# CrashMatch

### Your Road to Recovery Starts Here

**After the Crash, We Find the Right Match.**

---

## Overview

CrashMatch is a comparison platform inspired by Trivago that helps drivers find and compare accident-related services after a vehicle collision.

Instead of spending hours researching providers, users can compare trusted professionals in one place, including attorneys, insurance claim assistance providers, and collision repair shops.

CrashMatch simplifies the recovery process by providing transparent information, ratings, pricing estimates, and service comparisons to help users make informed decisions.

---

## Features

### Lawyer Comparison

- Ratings and reviews
- Areas of expertise
- Contingency fee percentages
- Case experience
- Estimated settlement ranges

### Insurance Assistance

- Service pricing
- Customer ratings
- Average claim processing times
- Coverage review services
- Claims support options

### Body Shop Marketplace

- Estimated repair costs
- Repair timelines
- Warranty offerings
- Certifications
- Customer reviews

### User-Friendly Dashboard

- View service comparisons
- Save favorite providers
- Access accident resources
- Organize recovery-related information

---

## Vision

CrashMatch aims to simplify the often overwhelming process that follows an auto accident by creating a single destination where users can compare services, make informed decisions, and connect with the resources they need to recover.
